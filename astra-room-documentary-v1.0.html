The ask

Follow the project from first prompt to finished room

The proposed film begins in the untouched room and follows the plan all the way through survey, trades, procurement, construction, commissioning, performance, and invoice closeout.
Greg Spero is a working musician and studio owner who began this room project without design or construction experience. He did know how musicians use the room, what a session needs, and when an image felt wrong. Through a long correction loop, he used Astra to turn an approved visual concept into an inspectable planning system: recorded geometry, editable 2D and 3D models, buyable furniture research, a line-item budget, trade scopes, material kits, ordered installation steps, and release gates for facts that still need people on site.
Proposed format: a 25-minute observational documentary, 16:9 master, clean master, full captions, transcript, trailer, cutdowns, production stills, and a public source appendix. OpenAI is asked to evaluate whether to follow or develop the story and to identify an editorial contact for a first conversation. If there is interest, TRC and a production partner would agree on access, production scope, editorial roles, distribution, and a quoted full-film budget. Production resources or support are an optional discussion, not a request approved by this document. No OpenAI participation, funding, or endorsement is claimed.
  • Room project cap: $25,000 all-in, separate from documentary financing.
  • Baseline price revision 1.7, checked 2026-09-28: $24,948.52 balanced; $24,953.78 ceiling-first; $23,257.16 with more cost protection.
  • The room ledger includes a $1,000 evidence-capture allowance for an archive and rough assembly. The finished 25-minute documentary needs a separate production agreement and quoted budget.
  • The final film is a completed case only after construction, commissioning, musical use, and cost records exist.
Why OpenAI

A visible chain of responsibility

The camera can show where an agent expands a user’s reach and where authority returns to the person, the trade, the site, or the receipt.
  • Astra organizes records, compares sources, researches products, debugs geometry and code, generates or revises planning images, assembles budget logic, and keeps unresolved facts visible.
  • Greg decides the room’s purpose, musical use, taste, acceptable compromises, spending cap, and whether a result works for people in sessions.
  • Bounded GPT workers perform isolated research, extraction, implementation, testing, or review. Astra integrates and judges the work.
  • The approved September 13 concept came from Greg’s earlier room work with Claude. The film credits that source and does not present the concept as Astra’s original creation.
  • Qualified people measure, engineer or approve loads and attachment details, quote, fabricate, install, inspect, and commission. A model does not certify a wall, circuit, overhead assembly, or field fit.
The errors remain in the film because correction is the evidence. Viewers see a musician use an agent to plan beyond his formal training while keeping human control over taste, money, physical facts, and safety.
Premise

Will the physical room accept the plan?

A musician pushes Astra past a bad first model and a beautiful but incomplete concept, then follows the resulting plan into a real $25,000 build where measurements, trades, receipts, and music decide what survives.
The stakes are already present. The first 3D pass is poor. The room shape and door assumptions change when old plans surface. The sofa direction changes after price, availability, shape, and fit review; the concept-faithful custom configuration still lacks a final dealer quote. Two raised storage-door platforms remain missing from the usable record and must be measured before fit can be released. The balanced budget comes within $51.48 of the cap. A wrong dimension can move furniture, change quantities, or stop custom work.
The ending stays conditional. If the room opens, the film compares the installed result with the model and records musicians using it. If a feature is deferred, the film shows the change and reason. If the build pauses, the documentary ends on the verified limit rather than manufacturing a reveal.

Astra during construction is a proposed future workflow. It has not happened yet.
  • The crew submits labeled photos, measurements, delivery records, and the condition found.
  • Astra compares them with the versioned plan, identifies affected scopes, and requests missing facts instead of guessing.
  • The qualified trade chooses and releases the physical method, attachment, circuit, fabrication detail, or repair.
  • Astra updates the drawing, quantity, order tracker, budget, dependency, and change log.
  • Greg approves the cost and design tradeoff within the $25,000 cap before work or purchase proceeds.
  • The installer completes the work; the crew captures the check; Astra logs the as-built result, invoice state, and remaining holds.
Possible scenes include a raised platform dimension changing a route or fit check; wall backing for five mirrors or the TV requiring a trade detail; a stock item becoming unavailable; or an on-site clearance missing the model. Capture them only if they occur.

Observed tension

The project already contains the conflict

No producer needs to invent a crisis.
  • The poor first 3D has visible orientation, scale, shape, and relationship problems.
  • Room polygon, non-45-degree returns, doors, booth, and object axes change what fits and how quantities calculate.
  • A concept-faithful custom sofa has uncertain price, lead time, configuration, and orderability; lower-cost candidates compromise curve, arms, upholstery, or size.
  • The balanced package leaves $51.48 after current reserves; ceiling-first leaves $46.22; reserve-protected leaves $1,742.84. Accepted quotes may force deferral.
  • Two raised storage-door platforms need width, depth, height, edge, and door-clearance measurements before fit is released.
  • Mirror retention, overhead support, fire performance, electrical compatibility, backing, and final attachments require qualified trade or engineering judgment.
  • The budget is a planning ledger. Only quotes, purchase records, changes, and invoices establish actual cost.
Direction

Let the room and the records carry the film

No fake terminal typing, glowing AI interface, or omniscient model voice.
Lock one 4K 16:9 master camera before any room change and recreate it after commissioning. Record lens, height, distance, tilt, white balance, lighting state, and time-of-day window. Keep the before grade honest. Native screen recordings show source changes; cameras show Greg deciding, trades measuring, and the room changing.
  • Use five fixed evidence labels: RECORD, MODEL, VISUALIZATION, FIELD VERIFIED, PENDING.
  • Every cost carries its version and status: observed price, allowance, accepted quote, purchase, or invoice.
  • Build the soundtrack from room tone, tape pulls, laser beeps, keys, drums, saws, drivers, and relay clicks. Capture 60 seconds of room tone at every phase.
  • Do not grade the before footage to look worse. Do not substitute a visualization for installed-room photography.
  • Use native screen capture when a readable interface matters. Record Greg and trades with independent clean audio.

Field plan

Shoot by milestone, protect the source record

The dates follow the real construction calendar. A missed moment is logged, not silently reenacted.
  • Phase A, now through survey: recover prompt history; lock story and source map; capture untouched room, first interview, native screens, and model versions; collect releases; photograph all surfaces and access points.
  • Phase B, trade development and procurement: film M-01–M-16, mockups, samples, redlines, scope choices, bids, final selections, quote revisions, and order releases.
  • Phase C, construction: capture demolition, deliveries, concealed conditions, inspections, changes, closure, installation, and commissioning. Seek 48 hours notice for irreversible events when the real schedule allows.
  • Phase D, closeout: matched final views, controlled performance, final interviews, punch list, as-built records, and actual-invoice review. Picture lock follows fact and rights checks.
Name media YYYYMMDD_PHASE_CAMERA_SHOT_TAKE.ext. Before any card is cleared, copy it to two separate verified backups and update the shot/asset register with source, creator, capture date, consent, rights, privacy, evidence state, intended edit use, and both backup locations. Keep one backup physically separate from the working drive.
Obtain appearance releases and location permission. Redact private prompts, account data, contacts, addresses, serial numbers, payment data, unreleased commercial terms, and sensitive security details. Make redacted review exports of readable screens. At fact check, audit every model role so Astra work is not assigned to Claude, earlier Claude concept work is not assigned to Astra, helper work is not collapsed into a single session, and trade judgment is not assigned to AI.

Picture lock

The documentary finishes when the evidence finishes

  • Trace and clear the earliest displayed prompt and every later direct quote.
  • Credit the approved concept to earlier Greg/Claude work; describe Astra and bounded GPT roles accurately.
  • Document survey, qualified trade releases, accepted quotes, procurement, construction, commissioning, and punch-list status.
  • Label every model, render, and visualization when confusion with the installed room is possible.
  • Complete the matched before/after camera record and controlled musical performance.
  • Use accepted quotes, changes, purchases, and invoices for planned-versus-actual costs; state exclusions.
  • Remove unsupported time-saved, ROI, acoustic, code, engineering, or OpenAI-endorsement claims.
  • Pass consent, privacy, brand, music, product-image, plan-record, and location clearance.
  • Deliver captions, transcript, masters, cutdowns, stills, appendix, project archive, and two verified backups.
The immediate decision is whether OpenAI wants to evaluate the story and name an editorial contact. Evidence capture should begin before the first site measurement or room change because the untouched room, concealed conditions, and first reactions cannot be recovered later.
