Capture the facts before the room changes

The locked before angle, room-use footage, native screen recordings, and first interview are the immediate priorities.
Before-state day

Room

  • Mark tripod feet, height, focal length, tilt, lighting state, and time of day.
  • Film eight corners, booth link, raised areas, ceiling, services, gear, doors, and current use.
  • Record a slow walkthrough in both directions and 60 seconds of room tone.

Process

  • Interview Greg in the current room.
  • Record the guide, early 3D, concept, live 3D, visualization, products, and packet natively.
  • Slate every recording with version and date.
Field survey and mockup

Measurements

  • Keep laser value, datum, and measured location visible in one shot.
  • Complete M-01 through M-16, including both raised storage-door areas.
  • Record every discrepancy between record, model, and field.

Fit

  • Tape furniture bodies and occupied envelopes.
  • Test doors, chair swivel, appliance service, instrument removal, and delivery routes.
  • Interview Greg and survey lead beside the marked plan.
Trade development

Decisions

  • Film mirror, finish, light, fabric, slat, rug, and furniture samples.
  • Capture redlines, shop drawings, loads, attachments, controls, and cutouts.
  • Show the dated evidence when a custom item is released.

Interviews

  • Ask the lead builder what changed after measuring.
  • Ask a glazier or millworker which physical detail controlled the work.
  • Keep final quote and invoice totals off screen until verified and cleared.
Construction and reveal

Build

  • Film protection, retained-equipment logs, timelapse, and every concealed condition before closure.
  • Capture one wide, one medium, and one detail for each major scope.
  • Record tests, punch list, as-built labels, and real site corrections.

Reveal

  • Recreate the locked before angle before anyone enters.
  • Capture Greg's first unscripted walkthrough.
  • Compare before, concept, 3D, visualization, and installed room with fixed labels.
Interview map

Greg

  • Film before work, after survey, and after reveal.
  • Ask what he knew about the room, what failed first, and why he kept pushing.
  • Define his design and construction inexperience without diminishing his music and studio experience.

Trades and process

  • Ask the lead builder what changed after the field survey.
  • Ask one specialty trade which physical detail controlled fabrication.
  • Show Astra's workflow through sourced evidence, not a scripted endorsement.

The treatment feels like a marked-up studio binder

Picture

  • Hard cuts reveal version changes.
  • Failures stay on screen long enough to inspect.
  • Real before footage keeps its natural texture.
  • No generic AI glow, particles, or fake typing.

Sound

  • Cable clicks, keys, drums, tape pulls, laser beeps, saws, and relays build rhythm.
  • If cleared music is used, it drops out under “you still have to do better.”
  • Dialogue leads the web mix.
  • Captions ship with the hero and both cutdowns.

Graphics

  • Barlow Condensed for chapters.
  • IBM Plex Mono for timecode and proof.
  • Five labels only: record, model, visualization, field verified, pending.
  • Every number cites a dated version.
