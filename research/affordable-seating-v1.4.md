## Recommendation for the next 3D update

Use the **Jennifer Taylor Home Alpine 90-inch sofa in Ebony Black boucle** with **two IKEA DYVLINGE chairs in Kelinge black**.

- Sofa: **$1,252.99**, manufacturer says **ready to ship in 5 business days**, 90 W x 36 D x 27.5 H in, 17.5-in seat height. The official width drawing says 90.5 in, so use 90.5 in for clearance. Oak frame, solid oak wedge feet, six-gauge sinuous springs, removable cushions, 600-lb capacity, and 40 manufacturer-site reviews make this a more credible purchase than the cheaper marketplace half-moon.
- Chairs: **$199 each / $398 pair**, 24.75 W x 29.5 D x 26.75 H in, 16.875-in seat height. The armless black shell and exposed chrome star base are the closest low-cost match to the approved concept and leave room for a guitar.
- Coordinated furniture total: **$1,650.99 before tax and any delivery upgrades**.
- Against the earlier selected HAY chairs at $3,295 each, the DYVLINGE pair costs **$6,192 less**. There is no published Craftmaster quote in the project, so no sofa savings are claimed.

The Alpine's front line is gently curved rather than a full half-moon. Its continuous low black shell, rounded arms, and warm oak feet still preserve the concept's dark sculptural mass and wood accent. The DYVLINGE upholstery is textured polyester fabric, not leather or faux leather. Its low 16.875-inch seat should be tested in person.

## Modeling dimensions for the winner

- Alpine body: use **90.5 W x 36 D x 27.5 H in** as the conservative envelope. Set the seat top at **17.5 in**, seat depth **24 in**, arm/back top **27.5 in**, visible wood feet **4 in**. Preserve the two separate seat cushions, continuous rounded back/arms, and triangular oak wedges at all four corners.
- DYVLINGE body: **24.75 W x 29.5 D x 26.75 H in**. Set the seat top at **16.875 in** and seat depth **18.5 in**. Preserve the armless reclined shell and exposed chrome five-leg star swivel base. Do not texture it as leather.
