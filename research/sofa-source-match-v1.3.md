# Sofa source match v1.3

Checked 2026-09-27. This pass replaces the rejected Mylene with a sofa chosen from the original approved concept's form: shallow crescent plan, three visibly separate seat cushions, three loose boxed back cushions, low rounded arms, an upholstered front rail, and dark patterned fabric.

## Selected: Craftmaster C9 Conversation Sofa C914256

**Model the C914256 at 112 W × 38 D × 37 H in, arm height 25 in, seat height 20 in.** The code resolves to Panel Arm 1 + Loose Boxed Back 4 + Tapered Leg 2 + Conversation Sofa 56. Craftmaster's current program page shows that exact C914256 room scene and a current order builder for the same options. Its April 2026 tearpad lists the conversation frame at 106–112 W × 38 D × 37 H depending on arm. The published C914256 configuration is 112 in wide. Older dealer data sometimes gives 39 or 40 in depth; the current manufacturer tearpad's 38 in controls.

This is the closest verified current source because the silhouette is already correct before changing fabric: shallow conversation arc, three distinct loose seats, three loose boxed backs, rounded low arms, and a conventional upholstered rail. It is 2 in wider than the requested 90–110 in target band, a smaller mismatch than replacing the intended sofa with a smooth bench or tub. In the recorded 146.25 in north span, the nominal remainder is 34.25 in before clearances and field verification.

The modeled finish is **Robert Allen Revive / Obsidian, SKU 179199**, a black-on-charcoal damask supplied as proposed customer-owned material (COM). Its tonal scrolling pattern is closer to the approved image than Craftmaster Adios-45's high-contrast trellis or Topanga-45's geometric pattern. Neither of those two Craftmaster fabrics is selected. The Revive manufacturer listing offers yardage at $66/yard with a two-yard minimum and a $5 memo sample. Its product data marks the yardage variant available, but this does not establish the quantity in stock. Confirm the required yardage with the dealer before ordering.

**This exact frame/fabric pairing is proposed, not factory approved.** Craftmaster's published COM policy requires a qualifying dealer with $50,000 annual business, 54 in wide fabric, and Grade 10 pricing. Revive is listed at 54–55 in wide, with a 13.5 in horizontal × 22.5 in vertical repeat; the dealer must confirm usable width, suitability, pattern cutting, yardage, price, and lead time. The 3D finish is a visualization of that pairing. A generative concept image cannot supply a uniquely identifiable upholstery SKU.

The finished C914256 is a custom-order product. Craftmaster supplies a live builder that generates an order form for a retailer; the manufacturer does not publish direct-cart inventory, a current retail price, or a guaranteed lead time. A retailer quote and physical fabric swatch remain required before ordering.
