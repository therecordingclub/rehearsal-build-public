# Room procurement budget v1.6

Audience: Greg, studio assistants, purchasing coordinator and room trades.

The accepted warm, dark room remains the visual reference. The budget reads like a field purchasing ledger: one large working total, source evidence beside every price, grouped line items, quantities, trade ownership and visible release conditions. Warm brass is the navigation accent; amber means an estimate or field hold. No invented precision is presented as a vendor quote.

The room image links directly to its mirror, ceiling, wall, lighting, desk, bar, lounge and flooring costs. All installation-kit materials must map to priced lines, retained items, included hardware or explicit alternatives. Shared drivers and rack components are counted once.

Users can change quantities and quote prices locally, see the total update and export their working CSV/JSON. A reset affects only this budget's overrides. The printed version expands the full ledger. Existing room arrangements, measurement notes and installation progress remain separate and untouched.

Completed buying research does not release field-dependent cut sizes, anchors, electric circuits or custom glass. The page distinguishes current advertised prices, independently estimated consumables, custom fabrication allowances and retained inventory. Budget reserves for tax, delivery and contingency remain editable and explicitly separate from quoted costs.
