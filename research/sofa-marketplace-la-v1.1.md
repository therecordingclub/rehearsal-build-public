## Dimension rules

- W × D × H always refers to overall outside dimensions, except where a seat dimension is explicitly named.
- Only Audrey supplies both outside width and depth without calling them estimates. Those dimensions still need measurement before purchase.
- The [Wayfair Ritz model page](https://www.wayfair.com/furniture/pdp/willa-arlo-interiors-ritz-855-velvet-curved-sofa-w005930997.html) and [Kardiel Alexis model page](https://www.kardiel.com/alexis-89-fabric-sofa-blanc-boucle/) supply conditional model dimensions. Neither listing has a confirmed model label. The Kardiel reference page shows different upholstery; the picture retains the seller's black sofa.
- Unknown sizes stay null in the dataset. Their picture assumptions are stored separately, and the plan omits a sofa footprint.
- Plans use an outside rectangle, not an exact curved outline. Conditional dimensions use dashed outlines. AI room pictures cannot prove dimensional accuracy.
- Room controls remain the planning record: north 146.25 in, west 259.5 in, maximum width 188 in. The east bay, raised door areas, delivery route and field fit remain unverified.
