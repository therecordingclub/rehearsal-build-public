## Why this pair

The Mylene is the closest orderable sofa found to the approved concept within the stated envelope. It has a real bowed front and curved back, one continuous tight seat, a low 27.75-inch profile, and a 97.25-inch width. Nomad Charcoal supplies the dark, visibly textured surface in the reference while keeping depth below the 44-inch limit. The exact CB2 front render is visibly lighter under its white studio lighting; the retailer's exact Nomad Charcoal swatch is the color reference.

The two AAL 81 Soft chairs reproduce the concept's most specific cues: compact organic shells, low backs, black leather, and exposed four-star swivel bases. At 30 by 28.75 inches each, their body footprints are materially tighter than the 35–38-inch CB2 leather swivel alternatives.

## Geometry handoff

- Model the sofa body at **97.25 W × 39.25 D × 27.75 H inches**. Its silhouette is genuinely curved; do not substitute the earlier Fitz envelope.
- Model two chair bodies at **30 W × 28.75 D × 32 H inches** each. Seat height is **14.25 inches** and seat depth is **21.75 inches**.
- Against the recorded 146.25-inch north-wall width, the sofa's nominal width leaves **49 inches total lateral remainder**. That is arithmetic fit only, not an aisle or code clearance.
- The recorded main-room length is **259.5 inches**. The 188-inch dimension is the maximum room width at the east bay; it is not the bay's length. Furniture positions and rotations are checked against the complete polygon rather than subtracting unrelated overall dimensions.
- Two chair bodies total 60 inches of width before spacing. Their bases rotate, so layout checks need a swept envelope and hand clearance beyond the static 30-by-28.75-inch rectangles.

These checks establish that the bodies can fit the recorded envelope. They do not replace field verification of the room polygon, doors, delivery path, HVAC/closet access, or final circulation.

## Before any order

1. Order or inspect the free Nomad Charcoal swatch. The CB2 studio render reads much lighter than the retailer's exact swatch, and the concept target is dark charcoal.
2. Recheck both exact configuration pages with the real delivery ZIP and confirm landed price, lead time, return terms, and whether custom upholstery is final sale.
3. Field-measure the room, delivery route, door widths, turns, elevator/stair constraints, and final door/HVAC/closet clearances.
4. Tape the sofa body and each chair's body plus rotation clearance on the floor. Keep the recorded room geometry provisional until that check is documented.
5. Confirm that the low 14.25-inch chair seat height is comfortable for the intended users before ordering two.
