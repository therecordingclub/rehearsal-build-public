## What is evidence, not field truth

The main polygon, 298.86 square feet and 102-inch ceiling came from Greg's May 2026 tape notes. They are measured-as-typed and unverified. A discrepancy over 2 percent triggers immediate escalation and redesign review. It is not permission to fabricate within a 2 percent tolerance. Smaller discrepancies still update every affected layout and order. The bay returns are the least reliable shell dimensions. The grandfathered existing acoustic fabric has no verified construction or performance rating, so the finish decision remains controlled.

The closet's nominal 34 by 46 inches, door locations, booth's nominal 10 feet 3 inches by 7 feet 11 inches and curved southwest wall come from a labeled plan. Pass 7 places D-3 on the west wall at z 223..259, hinged south and swinging in, and retains the booth trace behind a 29-inch vestibule. Field measurements must replace those trace coordinates before issue.

Door heights, slider makeup, wall and floor build-ups, ceiling structure, plenum, circuits, grounding topology, HVAC return and capacity, acoustic performance, outlets, tie lines and delivery access remain unknown.

## Coordination holds

1. **Door and booth geometry.** Superseded sources contain three versions. Use Pass 7 as the current source: D-3 is on the west wall at z 223..259, hinged south and swinging in; the south wall has only the booth slider. Field-survey D-2, D-3, vestibule, sliders and booth, then revise every downstream drawing. Delete D-1.
2. **Guitar wall.** The locked 5+5 arrangement has not been proven under the 102-inch ceiling. Trace the actual ten instruments on a full-size wall template before blocking or hanger release.
3. **PMC mains.** Inventory says IB1; the current model says IB1S. Read the plates and measure the pair and stands.
4. **NU1X.** Yamaha lists 59.125 W × 18.25 D × 40.3125 H inches, 1.625 inches wider than the brief and Pass 7 source. Holding the source north edge at z 3 moves the center to z 32.5625 and south edge to z 62.125; the bar starts at z 70.125 only under the source-derived proposed 8-inch gap. Inventory lists a Clavinova. Confirm the physical instrument and gap before quote, power, bar or delivery planning.
5. **Booth drums.** Photos are described as Yamaha while inventory records Gretsch Catalina. Audit the kit and measure the deployed setup.
6. **Main-room walls.** Current design removes grey fabric for plaster; older scope retains it. The fabric's construction and acoustic performance are unknown. Retain it until Greg releases a measured finish/acoustic decision.
7. **Lighting.** Recessed fixtures, DMX/control intent, current circuits and ceiling depth are unreconciled. Issue a reflected ceiling and control plan after field inspection.
8. **Overhead mirror.** The 96 by 96 inch mirror lacks verified glass, weight, backing, structure and attachment details. Hold fabrication and installation.
9. **Stale construction data.** An older 59-element published artifact observed during the initial review, old notes and the v0.1 build sheet describe obsolete scope; this is not a claim about the source project's current deployment. The initial 70-element snapshot is provenance only; frozen commit `1a1d56d` is the reviewed 69-element Pass 7 source. Mark all superseded files reference-only when the field-verified set is issued.
10. **Grandstage X.** Korg lists a 53.98 W × 18.15 D inch body, excluding the stand. The working model uses 53 × 14.6 inches. Measure the complete staged rig and rerun the D-2/HVAC fit.
11. **Bar refrigerator.** The Zephyr body is 23.375 inches deep and requires 1 inch behind it. The Pass 7 source's 24-inch bar remains 0.375 inch short before finish tolerance. Test the proposed 26-inch allowance and hold the order for shop-drawing and Greg approval.
12. **Dark floor.** Karndean SCB89 cannot be installed over a floating floor. Identify the existing oak assembly. Keep oak plus rugs as an explicit option; oak removal is proposed scope requiring Greg's written approval and preservation where feasible.
13. **Slat-panel height.** WoodUpp item 1029 is 94.49 inches high against a nominal 102-inch wall. Elevations must show the 7.51-inch joint, trim or approved custom condition without abandoning the 5+5 guitar layout.
14. **Corrected west-wall seating fit.** Four conflicts remain in the current proposal: the piano bench overlaps the source sofa envelope; the proposed 26-inch bar corner overlaps the unchanged sofa box by 2.86 × 7.875 inches; the north stool overlaps the sofa; and the south stool conflicts with the west swivel. Mock up the bench and sofa, bar corner, all three occupied stools, rotated occupied swivel and open fridge together; do not reduce the stool count or change the seating by default.
