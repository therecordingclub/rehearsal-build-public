## Mirrors

Five [BEAUTYPEAK YH2465G 24 × 65-in gold full-length arch mirrors](https://www.lowes.com/pd/BEAUTYPEAK-24-in-W-x-65-in-H-Arch-Gold-Framed-Full-Length-Floor-Mirror/5015053085) preserve the large five-arch wall at **$79.98 each**. Lowe's showed 725 available on the check date. Each mirror weighs 19 lb and includes bracket/hardware.

The approximate full-frame halo path is **13.97 ft each / 69.87 ft total / 80.35 ft with 15% gross**, assuming a proposed 12-in-radius semicircular top. This is a takeoff assumption, not a field measurement. Confirm one physical frame before cutting LEDs.

The $1,199.90 MIR-01 base includes the five mirrors, consumables and 12 paid installer-hours. The original custom-glass assembly remains an excluded quote-required alternate.

## Black acoustic-wall re-skin

The working fabric field is **475.32 sq ft before openings**: 609.92 gross wall sq ft minus 68.52 mirror and 66.08 slat footprints. At 15% allowance, 546.62 sq ft requires **34 linear yd** of 66-in-wide [Guilford FR701 Black 408](https://www.soundaway.com/FR701-Acoustical-Fabric-s/147.htm) at the observed **$43.15/yd**, or **$1,467.10**.

The $2,707.10 FIN-02 base comprises:

- 34 yd FR701 Black: **$1,467.10**.
- Measured edge, corner and penetration repair-component allowance: **$100.00**.
- 20 paid studio-assistant hours at $40 base: **$800.00**.
- Four skilled fabric-track supervisor hours at $85 base: **$340.00**.

No free crew is assumed. The assistant allowance represents two assistants for ten hours. The supervisor checks substrate and track condition, starts the crew, reviews tension and edges, and accepts the finished field. Stop for a field quote before buying if the retained core or track fails the grip, damage, moisture or compatibility checks, or if the system needs full specialist installation.

The recommended value-engineered purchase is two [Seafuloy LAF-XY2-Black 94.5-in solid-wood acoustic wall-panel four-packs](https://www.homedepot.com/p/337802144) at the observed **$122.44/set**, or **$244.88**. The $889.88 FIN-03 base comprises:

- Two Seafuloy four-panel sets: **$244.88**.
- New removable black penetration/access trim allowance: **$75.00**.
- Ten paid studio-assistant hours at $40 base: **$400.00**.

This substitutes for the installation kit's WoodUpp Akupanel item 1029. It changes the brand, module, surface detail and acoustic result, so approve one physical sample against the concept before release. The panels include a factory peel-and-stick provision, but the budget does not assume that adhesive can safely carry them over fabric or a weak acoustic core.

**Mandatory field-quote stop:** verify substrate, support, attachment, penetrations and removable access before ordering. If the retained wall/core/track cannot support the panels or the factory adhesive is unsuitable, price an approved backing and mechanical-fastening design first. Battens, ROCKWOOL and WoodUpp end lamellas remain conditionally priced but excluded from the base to avoid duplicating retained acoustic core or factory backing.
