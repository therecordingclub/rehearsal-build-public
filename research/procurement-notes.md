# Rehearsal room procurement notes

Verified against primary manufacturer sources on 2026-09-26. All listed prices remain `null`; obtain current written quotes, lead times, sample terms, freight, tax, warranty, and return terms before release.

## Corrections that affect the layout

- **Yamaha NU1X:** Yamaha's exact NU1X specification is **59.125 W × 18.25 D × 40.3125 H inches**, not the brief's 57.5-inch width. Preserve another **1.625 inches** in the bar/piano wall calculation. The exact NU1X is discontinued; the owned instrument must be field-measured. Do not silently substitute NU1XA dimensions.
- **Korg Grandstage X:** The body is **53.98 W × 18.15 D × 6.61 H inches**, excluding its music stand. The stand, bench, pedals, and cable bends still need a field footprint.
- **48-inch bar:** The Zephyr candidate is front-venting and only 15 inches wide, but its **23.375-inch body plus 1-inch required rear plug clearance equals a 24.375-inch minimum envelope before finish tolerance**. A nominal 24-inch-deep flush cabinet does not fit. Resolve with a deeper shop-drawn bar, a verified rear recess, or an intentionally proud appliance face.
- **Dark floor:** Karndean's rigid-core instructions require removal of laminate or any floating floor. If the current oak LVP floats, the proposed Ebony rigid-core floor cannot float over it.
- **Ten instruments:** CC01K is rated to 15 pounds and is not universal for wide classical or bass necks. Record each instrument's weight, neck width, headstock, and finish; assign CC01K or CC01 individually. Nitrocellulose can still mark under prolonged pressure even though String Swing says its padding does not eat the finish.

## Lighting coordination

- Use one 2700K visual family, with separate compatible controls and drivers for each load type. A shared control platform does not make tape drivers and downlights one electrical load.
- BLAZE 100 DI-24V-BLBSC1-27-016 is 24 VDC, 90+ CRI, 1.46 W/ft, and limited to **55 feet per feed**. Divide any longer perimeter into multiple fed runs; never daisy-chain past the manufacturer's run limit.
- Keep each OMNIDRIVE DI-ODBELV-24V96W-LPS at or below 96 watts. Locate drivers where they remain accessible and can dissipate heat. Record channel joints because S1 black channel stock lengths are 96 inches.
- Lutron identifies Caseta PD-5NE as ELV-capable and requires a neutral. Bench-test the exact dimmer, driver, tape length, and downlight load for low-end stability, flicker, pop-on, and audible noise before purchase.
- Nora NM4-RDC27BB supplies the requested black 4-inch, 2700K downlight. Release it only after a reflected-ceiling plan, cavity check, dimmer compatibility check, and in-room 2700K mockup.

## Acoustic wall package

- WoodUpp's **Class A statement is an acoustic absorption class**, not a fire-class claim. Its best published absorption configuration uses 45 mm battens and insulation, which changes wall depth and every junction.
- Reserve verified structural backing for all instrument hangers; coordinate that backing before acoustic slats or fabric-track work.
- Treat GeoTRAK, acoustic core, and FR701 as one submitted assembly. Component literature does not prove the completed wall's acoustic performance, fire performance, durability, or project compliance.
- Have the acoustician and installer detail slat-to-fabric transitions, outlets, doors, HVAC grilles, corners, removable access, fasteners, and fabric replacement. Approve black samples together under the actual 2700K lighting because their undertones and sheen will differ.

## Mirror safety boundary

- Omega's Dark Cloud, Smoky Quartz, and Gold Vein Overlay products are visual sample candidates. Order physical samples and approve a full-size mockup; handcrafted patterns vary.
- **Every overhead mirror panel and attachment must be engineered.** Require project-specific panel sizes, substrate assessment, safety backing or lamination, edge treatment, mechanical retention, access, and structural attachment details from the mirror fabricator and structural engineer.
- Do not use an adhesive-only ceiling detail. Do not infer overhead suitability or code compliance from a decorative mirror brochure. This research does not make a code-compliance assertion.

## Release sequence

1. Field-measure the NU1X, Korg setup, existing floor assembly, bar zone, ceiling cavity, wall substrates, and proposed instrument inventory.
2. Order finish samples for slats, fabric, flooring, mirror, channel/lens, and downlight; review them together under a working 2700K mockup.
3. Obtain coordinated shop drawings for the bar and refrigerator, wall backing and acoustic assembly, lighting feeds and controls, floor transition, and mirror retention.
4. Collect current written quotes and lead times only after the measured drawings and samples are approved.
5. Release nothing with an open structural, service-clearance, dimming, substrate, or assembly-test gate.
