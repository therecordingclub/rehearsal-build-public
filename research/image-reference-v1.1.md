## Arrangement read

The image centers a compact conversation group. A broad, shallow curved sofa sits against the north mirror wall. The NU1X and its long bench run along the west wall and face east. A low round table sits in front of the sofa. Both padded lounge chairs sit to the right and south of the table and turn inward; they are not a symmetrical pair placed side by side. The bar occupies the west foreground, south of the piano. The production desk stays on the east side at mid-room depth.

The reference piano and bench center at Z80 in, with the bar at Z160 in and stools at Z142/160/178 in. This keeps the piano visible at mid-left and the bar in the foreground, matching the visual hierarchy of the image. These are proposed positions; the door-swing fit check remains clear.

The `Image reference` arrangement encodes that read while retaining published product dimensions. It uses a proposed 100 × 40 in sofa envelope, reduced from the previous 46 in depth to reflect the shallower source silhouette. Coordinates and sofa size are visually inferred planning choices inside the current room polygon, not measurements taken from the perspective image. Previous layouts retain their own dimensions. The Korg, amps, PA allowance, booth kit, and booth desk remain in the complete-room plan even when the source camera does not show them.
