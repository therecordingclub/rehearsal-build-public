# Circular sofa retail research v1.0

Checked September 28, 2026. Prices are before tax and can change by ZIP. This is a retail shortlist for visualization and comparison; it does not change the selected FUR-036 sofa or authorize a purchase.

## Recommendation

**Render the Modway Resolute first.** It is the strongest black retail match to the approved crescent: the official angled photo shows a real inward conversation arc, black velvet, a continuous curved seat, and a gold plinth. Walmart showed the black SKU sold and shipped by Modway for **$1,337.78**, only **$84.79 above** the $1,252.99 Alpine baseline.

The tradeoff is footprint. At **100.5 W × 47.5 D × 30.5 H in.**, it adds 10 in. of width and 11.5 in. of depth. It must be tested with the M-12 tape/occupied-layout gate and M-15 delivery-route gate before selection.

## Evidence and fit notes

- [Modway's product page](https://modway.com/products/resolute-curved-performance-velvet-sofa-by-modway-eei-3408) supplies the exact dimensions, SKU, construction, weight capacity, and assembly status. Walmart supplies the lower live black-variant offer. The manufacturer's own inventory was not immediately available, so the Walmart seller/checkout path must be rechecked before purchase.
- [Furniture.com's Kendall listing](https://www.furniture.com/shop/kendall-chocolate-brown-velvet-sofa-tov-furniture-tov-l69004) ties the $1,599 offer to brown SKU L69004 and publishes 89.7 × 43.7 × 28.4 in. High Fashion Home independently showed the chocolate-brown 90 in. variant in stock.
- Wayfair publishes a 24 in. minimum door width for Shelly and labels it fully assembled. The Kendall is also fully assembled; its published carton is approximately 90.8 × 44.5 × 30.7 in. Neither fact proves the actual room route or turn clearances.
- All three exceed the Alpine's 36 in. depth. The approved room geometry does not establish that the deeper footprint works around occupied chairs, musician circulation, or the front projection. Render them size-accurately, then tape the exact envelope in the room before any selection.

## Rejected after visual inspection

- Home Depot Harcane S5873, Magic Home MH-S-139BK, and Walmart ReyTinn S5-P55 were inexpensive and described as curved, but their usable product photography reads nearly straight or only softly bowed. They do not answer the request for a stronger circular/crescent silhouette.
- Homary's 89 in. black Nordic curved sofa and Wayfair's inexpensive 90 in. half-moon listings were out of stock when checked.
- Search snippets and aggregator prices were not treated as live variant offers. No final delivery promise is made without a ZIP-specific checkout check.
