# Century Cornerstone LTD7600-2CRV source record

Checked 2026-09-27 against current Century manufacturer pages.

- Backup configuration: `LTD7600-2CRV`, Wide Half Round Arm, Loose Box Rounded Corners Back, three straight loose seats / three loose backs, upholstered base, 36 in frame-depth option.
- Geometry for comparison: **97 W × 43 D × 36–38 H in**. The 97 in width comes from Century's 36 in depth variable-width chart for the selected wide half-round arm. Century's current program listing gives the curved sofa's overall depth as 43–47 in and overall height as 36–38 in. A final dealer build sheet must resolve height before release-model fabrication.
- Order status: current configurable upholstery program with a live visualizer, fabric search, tear sheet, and dealer locator. It is made to order; Century does not show direct online stock, price, or lead time for this configuration.
- Fit assessment: the official image has three loose seats, three loose backs, and a shallow bowed front. The default photographed track arms and pale fabric are less faithful to the concept than the selected Craftmaster configuration; the wide half-round arm is available but not shown as a finished sofa in the downloaded image.
- Fabric: Century offers manufacturer fabric selection. No currently listed dark tonal damask was verified as an exact concept match, so no stocked fabric claim is made.
