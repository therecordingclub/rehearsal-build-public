# Craftmaster C9 Conversation Sofa source record

Checked 2026-09-27 against current Craftmaster manufacturer pages.

- Recommended configuration: `C914256` — Panel Arm 1, Loose Boxed Back 4, Tapered Leg 2, Conversation Sofa 56.
- Geometry for the room model: **112 W × 38 D × 37 H in**; arm height 25 in; seat height 20 in.
- Dimension confidence: 112 W is the published dimension for configuration C914256 and is consistent with the current manufacturer program's 106–112 in arm-dependent range. The current April 2026 Craftmaster tearpad specifies 38 in depth. Older dealer records sometimes say 39 or 40 in; use the current manufacturer value, 38 in.
- Construction visible in the official inspiration image: shallow crescent front, three loose seat cushions, three loose boxed backs, rounded panel arms.
- Order status: current made-to-order manufacturer program with an online builder and retailer submission path. The finished sofa is not represented as stocked. A retailer must quote price and lead time.
- Modeled finish: **Robert Allen Revive / Obsidian, SKU 179199**, proposed COM. Its dark tonal damask better matches the original image. See `../robert-allen-revive-obsidian/source.md` for price, repeat, fiber, and stock limitations.
- COM approval remains required: Craftmaster accepts eligible fabric through qualifying dealers; the dealer must confirm the 54 in usable width, suitability, pattern cut, yardage, final price, and lead time. This combination is not represented as a factory-approved or stocked SKU.
- Adios-45 and Topanga-45 assets are research alternatives only. Their trellis/geometric patterns were rejected for this visual direction.
- Finish: Black.
