## Sofa upholstery route

[Craftmaster's published COM policy](https://www.cmfurniture.com/faq/) accepts customer's own material through qualifying dealers, subject to fabric approval. It requires 54-in-wide fabric, prices COM at Grade 10, and excludes leather, vinyl, white/off-white, and plush velvet. Dealer eligibility requires $50,000 annual Craftmaster business. This cotton-blend damask is a proposed COM pairing, not a preapproved factory SKU.
